Research

My research connects perception, planning, control, and system integration for autonomous robots operating in challenging environments.

Uncertainty-aware informative path planning and learned navigation

Integrated learned collision and information-gain prediction for uncertainty-aware informative path planning, validated in real-world aerial-robot experiments [J1]. Related work includes motion-primitive planning, semantic-enhanced collision prediction, and risk-aware navigation [C2, C5, C8].

Control and aerial manipulation

Developed hybrid modelling and control for a reconfigurable aerial robotic chain in free flight and physical interaction modes [J4, C16], alongside collaborative work on shape and motion planning [C15]. First-authored a survey of model predictive control for micro aerial vehicles [C9].

Resilient perception and field autonomy

Contributed to complementary multimodal sensor fusion for robust localization in degraded subterranean environments [C14]. Field robotics work spans collision-tolerant aerial platforms, autonomous exploration and infrastructure inspection [C1, C6, C10, C11, C19, C20].

Multi-robot systems and hardware integration

Contributed to teamed exploration with legged and aerial robots [C4, C7] and to a self-deployed wireless communications system for subterranean robots [C18]. In Team CERBERUS, co-responsible for the autonomy stack of collision-tolerant aerial robots and developed LiDAR–camera calibration software.

Research interests

Embodied AI and autonomous systems; model predictive control; uncertainty-aware path and motion planning; multi-aerial-robot systems; aerial manipulation; multimodal SLAM and state estimation; robot learning and vision-language navigation; field robotics; robotics hardware and system integration.
